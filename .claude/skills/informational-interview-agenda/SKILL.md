# Skill: Informational-Interview Agenda (LaCivita)

> **When:** any conversation that is NOT a straightaway interview for a known open role, a warm
> intro that turned into a call, a "happy to chat" reply to a boss-hunt email, a referral
> conversation, a coffee chat. This is the single most likely OUTCOME of the warm rungs of the
> relationship-distance ladder, and it is easy to leave completely unplanned for.

**The frame:** *"If you are in an 'informational' interview, treat it the same way you would any
job interview."* The one difference is that it runs in **both directions**: it is also a networking
event, so the user leaves with names, not just goodwill.

## The agenda, in order

**1. Get them talking about their challenges FIRST.**
Open with *"What challenges are you currently facing?"* / *"What are your greatest obstacles right
now?"* Do not lead with background. Their needs are the focal point everything else attaches to.
> *"Every individual and every organization have gaps. Something is broken or stuck or there are
> aspirations for growth. Find out up front what they are!"*

**2. Offer ideas, solutions, and approach, scoped to what they just said.**
Show how what the user knows fits what they need **now**. Deliberately skip strong areas of their
background that are not what the other person needs at the moment. Even if nothing comes of the
conversation, they have given the other person something usable, and that has value on its own.

**3. Address any mismatch directly if there is no role to hire into.**
Too senior, too junior, wrong shape: say so and reframe:
> *"I join a company. I don't join a job."* — *"I'm interested in your organization. Even if I'm
> overqualified for your available positions, I'm thinking long-term."*

**4. Networking: get AND give.**
> *"I'd love to do some networking with you and perhaps I could refer you to people you might need
> on your team."* Then: first choice is working with you; if there's no spot right now, **who
> should I meet?** *"Does anybody come to mind? Anyone else? Anyone else?"* (until exhaustion), then
> prompt concretely: *"For example, do you know anybody at [3 named companies]? It doesn't need to
> be a bullseye."* Then **ask what referrals THEY need and give names.**

**5. More referrals, widen past their own team.**
> *"Are there any other types of people you need at your company? Individuals you always have a
> rough time finding?"* Then give names. *"Let them sort it out. Just give."*

**6. The recap.**
Close by confirming **the user's to-dos and their to-dos** out loud. This is not politeness: it
manufactures a legitimate reason to follow up.

## Guardrails (these override the agenda where they conflict with the user's own rules)

- **The three named companies in step 4 must clear dedup first**, so the user never asks for an
  intro to a company already off-limits or already contacted.
- **Any employer, contact, or leadership tier the user has excluded stays excluded**, in either
  direction: not as a referral asked for, not as a name given.
- **Honesty guardrails apply verbally too**, not just in writing. A spoken overclaim is not
  recoverable the way a draft is; hold every figure and every claim to the same standard as a
  written message.
- **The user is allowed to want less.** A lower-comp role with better work-life balance is a
  positive trade, not a compromise to apologize for; a salary floor is the floor for an offer, not a
  filter on a conversation.
- **Deal-breakers still apply** even at this warm depth: whatever the user's hard filters are
  (work-arrangement, industry, ownership structure, leadership politics), they hold here too. The
  rest of a deeper culture screen does not need to run at this depth; screen depth follows the ASK.
- **Log it.** The conversation and any names given go to the correspondence log and outreach log,
  and each name the user offers to pursue gets an armed follow-up date, same as a send.

## Why this is honor-system

There is no script boundary between the user and another human in a live conversation, so nothing
here can be automatically enforced. The mechanizable parts are the **dedup check on the three named
companies** and the **follow-up arming afterward**, both of which can route through the kit's
existing dedup and follow-up tooling.
