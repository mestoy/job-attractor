# The pairing model: approaches first, three pre-vetted options, iterate to your approval

An example operating-model ruling this pattern is built from, verbatim core: *"Your role is to pair with me. You are to suggest 3 pre-vetted approaches... I will select the approach or provide one, and we will discuss until I select one. From there, we will determine how to construct the message. Again, you will provide 3 pre-vetted options and I can provide optional suggestions. We will continue iterating until I approve a final. This is our durable operating model for all communication and writing unless I say otherwise."*

Adopt this skill by making that ruling your own (or writing a variant of it) and keeping it durable until you say otherwise.

## The procedure, in order, no skipping

1. **APPROACH ROUND (no text drafted here).** Present exactly 3 pre-vetted APPROACHES: concepts grounded in your experience (the profile skill) and the recipient's own material (the JD, the post, the thread). Pre-vetted = the approaches are scored by a panel of relevant seats (e.g. a CPO/CTO/CEO read) BEFORE you see them, present ranked descending with scores and each seat's one-liner. You select one, supply your own, or open a discussion thread. **The discussion continues until YOU say selected.** A picker option for "discuss first" is always present.
2. **CONSTRUCTION ROUNDS.** Only after the approach is selected. For each structural call and each beat: 3 pre-vetted options (panel-ranked and refined toward strong before they reach you), plus Other for your suggestions. Your picked words are yours; never rewrite them without returning the change as an option.
3. **ITERATE TO APPROVAL.** Assembled wholes go back to you for approval before any lint-driven or reviewer-driven change is applied. Panel or reviewer fixes to text you approved return as OPTIONS with the fix named, never silently folded. Nothing compiles, stages, or sends on assembled-but-unapproved text.

## Guardrails that ride every round

- Ground options in your sent samples for the right register (`scripts/voice_samples.py <type>`), the honesty guardrails (profile skill, HARD-INVARIANTS), and house-style (the `house-style` skill).
- When the recipient published a structure (criteria list, success plan), at least one approach mirrors it — a list-shaped recipient calls for a list-shaped approach, and a cover letter mirrors the JD's own path.
- The linters (`check_style.py`, `check_outreach.py`) run on option text BEFORE it is shown, not after you pick.
- This model supersedes speed: "assemble from what is in hand" applies to research, never to your words.

## Enforcement

Advisory here; mechanical enforcement (if you build it) is a `check_preview` extension that blocks a picker carrying an assembled full message without an approach-selection ledger row for that target. Until that gate ships, this skill is the reader.

## Your words first (a same-day refinement some users add: "You should prompt me to write to an idea in my own words")

Every construction beat OPENS by prompting you to write the idea in your own words: name the idea plainly (the beat's job, the facts available), then invite your telling before or alongside the pre-vetted options. The options are scaffolding you can react to, never substitutes for your words; when you write, your text becomes the base and the panel vets IT (with fixes returned as options).

## Interview-first is a common DEFAULT (a same-day refinement some users add: "the interview option should be the default")

A strong default opening for every construction beat is a short INTERVIEW: 2 or 3 questions about the moment or idea (what you remember, how the decision arrived, what it felt like, what was said), grounded first in anything you've already documented on the topic. Your answers become the raw material; the panel's pre-vetted options appear after, as scaffolding against your words. Direct writing and options-first remain available when you ask for them.
