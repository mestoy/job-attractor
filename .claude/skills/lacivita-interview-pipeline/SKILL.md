# LaCivita interviewing, the stage pipeline

> **This file is an INDEX, not a transcript.** Andy's original wording lives in his published
> books and his purchased course material (©milewalk). These files capture each stage's purpose,
> shape, and mechanics so you can build your own answers in your own voice. Nothing here is a
> script to recite, and none of it replaces his material.

## The spine

The method rests on one idea: **prepare stories, not answers.** You cannot predict the questions,
so you stop trying. Instead you build a small set of true stories from your own work, learn to
tell each one in a tight, repeatable shape, and then answer whatever gets asked by selecting the
right story and aiming it at the question. Confidence in the room comes from knowing your stories
cold, not from memorizing answers.

The second idea: **an interview is an exchange, not an audition.** You are evaluating them while
they evaluate you, and the questions you ask are scored as hard as the answers you give.

## The six stages

Work these in order for a new interview. Each stage has its own skill file with the shape,
the common scenarios, and how to build the candidate's script for it.

## Rules that bind every stage

- **The candidate's words, never the kit's.** Every script is co built with the candidate from
  their real experience, one beat at a time, through explicit choices. Never hand them a finished
  answer to approve; a recited answer sounds recited, and it is not theirs.
- **Truth only.** Every story, figure, and claim comes from the candidate's verified record. If a
  figure is not sourced, it does not go in a story. An interview answer is a claim you will be
  asked to defend.
- **Recency weighted.** Where Andy's older material and his newer material disagree, the newer
  wins. The stage files note where the method changed.
- **Whose search is this.** Nothing in these files assumes a particular candidate, seat, or
  seniority. Where seniority changes the method (it does, in stages 2 and 3), the stage file says
  how.

## When to load what

- Interview scheduled → load this index, then stage 1, and work forward.
- Interview inside 48 hours and stages 1 to 4 are built → jump to stage 5.
- Interview finished → stage 6, the same day.
- Building outreach, not interviewing → this pipeline is the wrong tool; use
  `lacivita-networking-templates.md`.
