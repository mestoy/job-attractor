# Stage 5 — day of, and the day before

> Index and ground rules: `lacivita-interview-pipeline.md`. Andy's original wording is not
> reproduced here; build the candidate's own in their voice.

## What this stage is for

By now the material exists: the opener, the rack, the routing map, the question sequence. This
stage compresses it into something usable under adrenaline and removes every avoidable failure
from the day itself. Preparation the candidate cannot access mid conversation is preparation that
does not exist.

## The day before: the rehearsal pass

- Say the opener aloud twice. Not read, said.
- Tell two stories from the rack aloud, picked by the routing map as most likely for this
  interviewer. Full version once, thirty second version once.
- Read the question sequence and its sources once, out loud, so the observations are in the ear.
- Re read the interviewer's profile and the company's latest public move; anything new since the
  research pass gets one line on the card.
- Then stop. Cramming past this point trades calm for coverage, and calm wins.

## The one page card

One page, large type, visible at a glance during a video call. It carries, in this order:

1. The opener's six beats as keywords, not sentences.
2. The rack: six story titles with their slot tags. Titles only; the stories live in the
   candidate, not on the page.
3. The routing map's top five likely question types with their story routes.
4. The question sequence, Company then Role then Boss, each with its one line observation.
5. The interviewer's name as pronounced, their title, and one human detail from research.
6. The logistics: exact time with timezone, the join link, a phone number fallback.

If it does not fit one page, it is not a card yet; cut until it fits.

## The video call checklist

- Camera at eye level, face lit from the front, the busiest part of the background cleared.
- Notifications off on every device, the machine plugged in, the join link tested in the actual
  browser it will open in.
- Join two minutes early, card positioned NEXT TO the camera so glancing at it reads as thinking,
  not reading.
- Water within reach. A pen and paper for THEIR answers; taking notes on the role's reality is
  both useful and visible respect.
- If the call drops, the recovery is scripted in advance: rejoin once, then the phone fallback,
  no apology spiral. Technical trouble handled calmly is itself a signal.

## In the room

- The opener is the only rehearsed delivery; everything else is selection. Trust the rack.
- Listen for the two or three moments where the interviewer says what is actually hard in the
  seat, and write them down; they feed stage 6 and every later round.
- Close by asking about next steps and the timeline, plainly. Wanting the job is not a secret.

Output: the one page card, built from stages 1 to 4, and a completed checklist before the call.
