# Stage 6 — follow up

> Index and ground rules: `lacivita-interview-pipeline.md`. Andy's original wording is not
> reproduced here; build the candidate's own in their voice.

## What this stage is for

The interview is not over when the call ends. A specific, same day thank you note extends the
conversation, proves the candidate was listening, and keeps them present while the panel compares
notes. Most candidates send nothing or send boilerplate; a real note is cheap differentiation.

## The thank you note, inside 24 hours

Same day is better than next day. The note is short, and it must carry:

1. **Thanks, one sentence, specific to the conversation.** Not "for your time."
2. **One detail from THEIR answers.** The moment stage 5 told the candidate to write down: the
   thing the interviewer said was hard, or the plan they described. Reflected back accurately,
   it proves listening and restarts the thought in their head.
3. **One connection to the candidate's fit.** A single sentence linking that detail to something
   the candidate brings. Not a re pitch, one thread.
4. **Forward motion.** Enthusiasm for the next step, said plainly.

What it must NOT carry: a recap of the candidate's answers, corrections of things they wish they
had said better, new claims, or length. Five sentences is a full note.

One note per interviewer when there were several, each with a different detail; identical notes
compare badly, and panels compare.

## The silence protocol

- If a timeline was given: nothing until it passes, then one short, warm check in referencing the
  stated date. Never before; chasing inside the window reads as anxiety.
- If no timeline was given: the thank you note's forward motion sentence asks about next steps,
  which usually surfaces one. If silence follows anyway, one check in after about a week, then
  route energy to new targets rather than a second chase. New conversations beat chased ones.

## The negotiation boundary

During interview rounds, the candidate's job is to build desire and defer money:

- If asked for a number early, deflect warmly and put the anchor back on them: the candidate is
  focused on fit, and trusts the company to make a fair offer for the level. Make them name the
  range.
- Never negotiate details before an offer exists; desire first, terms after.
- Log any figures THEY volunteer (a stated band, an equity mention) in the workspace's records
  the same day, verbatim, with who said it. Those become the negotiation baseline later.

## Building the note

1. Same day as the interview, pull the candidate's notes from the call (the stage 5 writedowns).
2. Co build the note beat by beat in the candidate's words: they pick the detail, they phrase the
   connection. Never draft the whole note for approval.
3. Run the workspace's style and honesty checks as with any outreach; a thank you note is still a
   message that represents them.
4. The candidate sends it themself, from their own account, and it gets logged like any send.

Output: a sent, logged thank you note per interviewer, inside 24 hours, plus any volunteered
figures logged verbatim.
