# Interview Preparation

> Template. Ready-made answer bank + protocols that `/interview` wires into a stage-specific prep pack. Fill the CAAR examples with your own real stories.

## Method: CAAR (not STAR)
Answer behavioral/experience questions as **Context · Approach · Action · Result**:
- **Context** — the situation and stakes.
- **Approach** — *how you thought about it* before acting (the judgment step; this is what STAR omits, and it lets you speak credibly about work a teammate executed).
- **Action** — what you actually did.
- **Result** — the outcome, with an honest, tenure-scoped metric.

## Your CAAR stories (fill in — 4–6 reusable ones)
For each: a short title, a "Use for:" tag (which question types it answers), and the C/A/A/R beats grounded strictly in facts from `01-candidate-profile.md`.

### [Story title] — Use for: [leadership / ambiguity / conflict / 0-to-1 / failure / …]
- **Context:** …
- **Approach:** …
- **Action:** …
- **Result:** … (honest metric)

*(Repeat. Cover the common themes: a 0-to-1 win, an ambiguous mandate, a conflict/disagreement, a failure + what you learned, a leadership/influence moment.)*

## The "you don't have [X]" pattern (for gaps)
Acknowledge honestly → connect the nearest adjacent experience → show the concrete learning path. Never invent experience.

## Tough questions (prepare per-application answers)
- "Why this company specifically?" (use a *verified* recent hook, never generic)
- "Why are you leaving / did you leave?"
- "What's your biggest weakness / a failure?"
- "Any reservations about this role?"
- "Salary expectations?"

## Questions to ask them (pick 4–6, by stage)
- **Role/team (screens):** What does success look like in 6 months? How is the team structured?
- **Tech/growth (technical rounds):** What's the stack / biggest technical challenge? How do you invest in people's growth?
- **Culture/leadership (final round — last chance to catch a deal-breaker):**
  - "Who on your team has been promoted, and what did you do to get them there?"
  - "Describe a decision someone in this role made that you disagreed with. What happened?"
  - "How do you know someone's doing good work when you can't see them working?"
  - "When two leaders disagree on direction, how does that get resolved?"

## Roleplay protocol (for the optional mock)
Warm-up → role-specific technical questions → 1–2 behavioral questions tied to the posting → one tough/curveball. Give brief feedback after each answer; coach toward *your* natural register (per `02-behavioral-profile.md`), not a generic ideal.

## Logistics tips
- Phone/video: test audio/video, quiet space, notes out of eyeline, have the CV and job posting open.
