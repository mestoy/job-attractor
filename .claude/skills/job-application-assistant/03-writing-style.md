# Writing Style

> Template. The rules the assistant follows when writing anything in your voice — cover letters, outreach, LinkedIn copy.

## Voice
- [First-person, active, plain and warm — adjust to you]
- [Understated, specific, concrete. Wit baked into plain statements, never announced.]

## Do
- Lead with the single strongest, most relevant point.
- Be specific and concrete; quantify where honest.
- Match the reader (a founder, an engineer, a product lead each hear it differently).

## Don't
- No em-dashes as sentence connectors if you consider them an AI "tell" (use commas, colons, periods).
- No filler ("very," "really," "actually"), no clichés, no over-hedging/apologizing.
- No announced-label openers ("Short version:", "Here's my pitch:").
- No jargon dumps or parenthetical credential lists.

## Length
- Outreach notes: short, a few tight beats. Cover letters (if used): ~1 page.
