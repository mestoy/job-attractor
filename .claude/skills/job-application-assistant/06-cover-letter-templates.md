# Cover Letter Templates

> Template. **Optional** — many candidates skip cover letters entirely and lead with a tailored résumé plus direct outreach. If you do use them, this defines the structure.

## When to use
- Only when a posting requires one, or when a short, specific letter genuinely strengthens the application. Otherwise, put that energy into `/radar-outreach` or `/matrix-hunt` outreach to the hiring manager.

## Structure (≈ 1 page)
1. **Opening** — tailored to the specific role and company; lead with your single strongest match. No generic "I am writing to apply for…".
2. **Body (1–2 short paragraphs)** — connect specific, real experience to the posting's top requirements. Acknowledge a genuine gap honestly and frame adjacent experience rather than hiding it.
3. **Why this company** — one specific, *verified* reason (a product, a mission, a stated priority), not flattery.
4. **Close** — brief, warm, confident.

## Rules
- Match the writing-style rules in `03-writing-style.md` and the voice in `02-behavioral-profile.md`.
- Address a named person if known, else "Dear Hiring Manager."
- Every company-specific claim must be independently verified before it goes in.
- Never claim a skill or achievement not in `01-candidate-profile.md`.
