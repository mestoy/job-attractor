## Scoring dimensions (weighted — rank, don't gate)
Suggested weights (tune to you):

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Technical / skills match | 30% | Do your skills map to the requirements? |
| Experience match | 25% | Does your history map to the role? |
| Behavioral / culture fit | 15% | Does the environment fit how you work? |
| Career alignment | 30% | Does it move you toward what you want? |

Location is scored as PASS / FAIL / FLAG (FAIL vetoes regardless of score).

## Company research checklist (used before recommending any company)
1. **Culture** — Glassdoor rating / %recommend / recent trend + recent positive & negative themes with real quotes.
2. **News (last ~12 mo)** — layoffs, PE cost-cutting, restructuring, lawsuits, leadership exits.
3. **Leadership stability + retention** — founder stable? healthy vs. dysfunction-driven turnover?
4. **Remote reality** — confirmed from the company's own listings.
5. **Deal-breakers** — re-confirm none are tripped.
