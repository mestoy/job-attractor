# Job Attractor Pipeline skill (generic starter)

Automate the funnel (discover → screen → research → draft) and **stop at a human gate before anything leaves the machine.** Fill `documents/PROFILE.md` first; that's your identity, target roles, hard filters, honesty guardrails, and voice.

## Step 0 — Personalize the voice, then build employer context (first run, then keep both fresh)
**0a. Gather writing samples from the very first use — and keep gathering.** Before producing anything in the user's name, actively ask for and ingest their writing samples: LinkedIn About, past emails/messages they've sent, essays, blog/portfolio copy, cover letters — anything in their own words. Distill the patterns (idioms, cadence, signature phrases, what they avoid) into `documents/writing-style-guide.md`. Then keep it continuous: every time the user edits a draft or sends a real message, fold the delta back into the style guide (the self-learning loop). **Everything the pipeline writes — narratives, outreach, collateral — comes FROM this voice, never a generic AI register.** This is what personalizes each pipeline.

**0b. Build your employer context.** Right after `/setup`, **walk the user through the `narrative-audit` skill for EACH employer in their history, one at a time.** It researches what each company officially published, checks the user's résumé/profile/LinkedIn/interview-prep against it (honesty deltas + richer source-backed detail + options), and **triggers a collateral refresh** so their employment history is accurate and compelling before any outreach goes out. Most candidates are missing this employer context — it's what turns vague claims into specific, defensible ones. **Re-run `narrative-audit` automatically whenever a new employer is added, and again at interview prep** (companies change). Then proceed to the loop below.

## Cadence & autonomy model
- **Target queue depth = 50 approval-pending matches; run continuously while below 50** (applies to ANY queue). Schedule the prep task to fire frequently (e.g. every 15 min on weekdays) and top the queue up toward 50 screened-clean NEW entries; idle when it already holds 50. Prepare, never send.
- **Queue hygiene: the live queue holds ONLY approval-pending (NEW) items.** When an entry is sent or dropped, move it to `documents/outreach-queue-archive.md` (records also live in your log/tracker). The queue stays a clean "needs you" list.
- **Always arm a follow-up after a send** (a daily sweep surfaces due value-add second touches for your review; never a cold connect).
- **Target volume = ~10 quality *initial* outreaches/day** (LaCivita's 90/1/9 funnel), **email first, résumé attached, one touch per medium.** Quality over quantity, not hundreds of cold sends.
- **You send (the human gate).** The pipeline prepares, queues, and, on your go, opens the prefilled email draft. **Never auto-send, auto-connect, auto-open email, or auto-submit.**
- Requires your OK to schedule the hourly task and any email/computer access.

## Collaboration style (how the pipeline should work with you)
- **One decision at a time, with the reasoning shown.** Screens run first; you get a clear recommendation and why, not a data dump.
- **Screen from data → structured Match Scorecard → PAUSE.** Sites open only after you confirm the match. Scannable cards/tables over prose.
- **Decision surface = interactive scorecard WITH decision buttons.** Present each scorecard/decision as a rendered card whose buttons are the choices (Prep / Deeper probe / Drop / Skip; or the beat-options during line-by-line), not prose plus a separate question. One decision at a time, still human-in-the-loop — just delivered as a card you click.
- **Background research at every gate pass + parallelize.** The moment a prospect clears a gate (hard filters → culture → Glassdoor → JD-verify → boss-target), immediately launch the NEXT research task for it in the background (boss public-footprint for praise, deeper probes, résumé inputs). While you decide on the current prospect, run the next one or two prospects' JD-verify in the background so every scorecard arrives decision-ready. Parallelize the gathering, never the deciding.
- **For any wording you'll send, get 3 options + a recommendation + a free-response "add your own."** Options come from your `writing-style-guide.md` (your voice) and pair the boss's specific accomplishment with a specific accomplishment of yours.
- **Line-by-line construction:** when building an outreach deliberately, don't hand over a whole draft, build the email one beat at a time (3 options per beat in your voice, honoring each beat's LaCivita function: greeting -> hook -> boss-praise -> offer -> ask -> sign-off), you pick each. Offer a single assembled draft only when you want speed.
- **Boss-praise = two-stage, human-in-the-loop (never the assistant's unilateral pick).** Stage 1: research the boss's public footprint and surface **3 accomplishments that align with your interests**, each with its source, for you to choose the angle. Stage 2: once you pick, get **3 phrasings of that compliment in your voice** to choose/edit. Only then finalize. Present via a multiple-choice prompt or the review console; never fire the mailto before both picks.
- **Close the loop, then tee up the next single step.** Flags surfaced honestly, even when you're leaning yes.

## The loop (each run)
1. **Discover** new matches (web search + any job scrapers you add). De-dupe against your tracker, **`documents/blocked-employers-list.md`** (grep it — keep it in the repo so both sessions read it), and already-contacted.
2. **Screen** each candidate **automatically and in full before it reaches your decision**, in this fixed order, stopping at the first fail: (a) **blocked/set-aside list** (`grep documents/blocked-employers-list.md`; if listed, DROP); (b) **hard filters** (`WORKFLOW-RULES.md`: remote/location, deal-breaker industries, layoffs, always-on, any personal hard constraints); (c) **culture/leadership/news** screen; (d) **Glassdoor** read (rating/WLB/CEO approval/recent themes; note if too small for a read); (e) remaining fit. **Only companies that clear a–d surface for your decision, each with its screen results attached.** Any fail = DROP with the reason; it never reaches you. Can't complete a screen? Flag it as an open item, don't pass silently.
2b. **Screen from data only (no tabs).** Do the screen via web search + known facts. **Do NOT open the boss's LinkedIn or the company site during screening** — sites open only after you confirm the match (avoids wasted tabs on companies that fail).
2b-remote. **When a hard filter like remote/location is unverified, run a DEEPER search before deciding:** their careers/ATS postings, Wellfound/BuiltIn/FlexJobs, and how they describe their work model (remote-first vs "hybrid, report to a hub"). Look for evidence in favor, and say that a deeper search was performed and what it found. Fully-remote → pass; hybrid/in-office → hard fail; unclear → surface as an open item.
2b-negatives. **When a screen surfaces a negative (culture/leadership flag, layoffs, turnover, adverse news), investigate DEEPER before deciding:** root cause, trend over the past ~12 months (improving vs regressing, dated reviews), and corrective actions (new leadership, policy changes, whether the problem people have left). Present the trajectory and context, not just the point-in-time negative. A problem being actively fixed is different from a festering one.
3. **Verify the live JD, THEN present a Match Scorecard, then PAUSE.** Before scorecarding, open the actual posting on the company ATS/careers and confirm from the JD text: remote + travel + hybrid/relocation (an aggregator "remote" tag is not proof), comp band, seniority/years bar, reporting line, and role/lane match. The scorecard reflects JD-verified facts; a gated JD means mark those facts UNVERIFIED. Then **present a Match Scorecard, then PAUSE.** Surface each screened-clean company as a **structured, scannable scorecard (not prose)** — Company · Boss (name/title/verified?) · Why match · Screen results (blocked/remote/industry/layoffs/Glassdoor) · Flags for you · Recommendation — and wait for your go before any deep-dive or tab-opening. A rendered card is ideal; a clean table is the floor. One company at a time unless you ask for the batch. **No open req is NOT a disqualifier:** boss-hunting communicates value to the boss regardless of a job opening (LaCivita). If a role exists, JD-verify it against the hard filters; if none exists, this is a value-reach (radar) — verify company-level remote/travel/industry/culture and reach out on value, with comp as a conversation-time item. "No live role" is a scorecard flag, never a drop; only company-level hard filters / deal-breakers / toxic-grindset-AI-mandate culture / instability drop a match.
4. **Only AFTER you confirm the match: pick + verify the boss and deep-dive** (now open LinkedIn + company sources) using the `boss-hunt-message` skill's SAFETY-GATED rules: verifiable, publicly self-disclosed accomplishments + genuine alignment hooks; **never infer identity**; draw attention to similarities positively.
5. **Write the "why this match" dossier** mapped to your `PROFILE.md` preferences.
6. **Draft the outreach EMAIL** via the `boss-hunt-message` skill (LaCivita structure + your voice + your honesty guardrails).
7. **Prepare the review package** — run the **`outreach-send-prep`** skill (mailto-prefilled draft + built-and-presented résumé; never desktop-control the compose window; never auto-send): (a) the **email draft** (To / Subject / Body) queued in `documents/outreach-queue.md` with the boss-praise source, and (b) a **company-tailored résumé** built to a consistent naming convention (e.g. `<Your Name> - Resume - <Company>.pdf`), **opened for your inspection**. Review BOTH before anything goes out; on your go, open the mail draft so you attach the résumé and send. Never send.
7b. **Seed** `documents/boss-hunt-learning-log.md` (match, boss, compliment + why, suggested draft); fill the actual-vs-suggested after you send (`documents/self-learning.md`).
8. **Log** the run's funnel numbers to `documents/outreach-metrics.md`.

## Safety & quality gates (all must hold)
- Human gate before send / connect / apply / email-open. Public information only; never infer identity; omit when unsure. Verify the boss's current role. Apply YOUR honesty guardrails (`PROFILE.md`). Escalate ambiguous screens to you rather than guessing.
- **Upstream defects get filed, not patched:** when a SHIPPED script or rule is broken, follow docs/partner-feedback-protocol.md (append to documents/partner-feedback.md, tell the operator); never silently edit shipped scripts.

## Ready-to-send takes priority (added 2026-07-17)
**Ready-to-send packages are the FIRST decision option.** Whenever one or more outreach packages are ready to send (person vetted + email pre-filled + résumé tailored), present SEND as the first choice, ahead of serving new scorecards, queuing a new person, or anything else. Finishing a ready package outranks preparing a new one; list all ready sends first if there are several.
